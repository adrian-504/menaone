# Interview guide: Finance

For the conversation with the Director of Finance. Ask the shared questions in [README.md](README.md) as well. **Finance covers two jobs** (owner, 23 September 2026), so this conversation has two parts.

## Part 1: MENA's own invoicing and collections

**What we think happens** (to be corrected): each month, every client is invoiced for what they have live. The shapes are:

- retainers by headcount band
- per-person Employer of Record fees
- Recruitment placement fees
- Business Setup milestones
- one-time work
- extras charged outside the retainer

1. Which accounting software issues the invoices? Is it certified for ZATCA e-invoicing?
2. Walk us through last month's invoicing. How do you know what to invoice each client, and who gives you each piece: headcount, placements, milestones, extras?
3. How many invoices go out in a month? What goes wrong most often: a wrong band, a missed extra, a late input?
4. Collections: how do you track who has paid and who is late? Who chases, and how?
5. Is there one list of which services each client has live right now? If not, what do you use instead?
6. What do you report to the owner each month, and how long does it take to put together?

## Part 2: the Accountancy & VAT service for clients

**What we think happens** (to be corrected): bookkeeping for clients, with or without VAT and e-invoicing. Packages differ for inactive companies ("No Projects") and operational ones ("Projects"). There are also VAT returns on their own.

7. Which clients are on which package? Where is each client's work done: in their system, in yours, in Excel?
8. What's the monthly and quarterly calendar (bookkeeping close, VAT return dates)? How do you make sure nothing is filed late?
9. What do you need from each client, and by when? How late does it arrive?
10. Who on the team handles which clients?

## Access

11. The owner and Hassan see everything; staff see their own work. What does Finance need to see from the other departments to invoice correctly? Is it everything, or only the figures (headcount, placements, milestones, extras)?

## Handovers (both parts)

12. From Payroll, Admin & PRO and Recruitment: what should reach you every month, and what's usually missing?

## The Director's view

13. At a glance, what do you want to see: this month's invoicing status, overdue payments, client filings due?

**Likely first pieces to build** (to confirm):

- a monthly "what to invoice" list for each client, drawn from the other modules
- the client filing calendar for Accountancy & VAT

The ledger and e-invoicing stay in the accounting software.
