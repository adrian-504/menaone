# Interview guide: Administration and PRO

For the conversation with the Director of Administration and PRO. Ask the shared questions in [README.md](README.md) as well.

**What we think the department does** (to be corrected in the conversation): government work for clients, including visas, iqamas, exit/re-entry, licence and CR renewals, Qiwa, Muqeem, Balady and the Chamber. It also runs Business Setup, Company Maintenance and the GM Representative service (owner, 23 Sep). It handles the visa and iqama side of Employer of Record, while Payroll handles the pay. Clients pay a monthly retainer, banded by headcount.

**This is the first module**, so this conversation goes deeper than the others.

## Requests

1. How does a client ask for something: email, WhatsApp, a call, a portal? Who receives it?
2. Walk us through last week's requests. Roughly how many, and which kinds come up most?
3. For each common kind: which steps does it take, which portals, and which documents from the client? How long does it usually take?
4. What makes a request get stuck? What do you wait for, and from whom?
5. How does the client learn it's done?
6. What falls outside the retainer and is charged on top? How do you record that so it gets invoiced?

## Expiries and renewals

7. Which client documents expire: CR, licences, iqamas, visas, Chamber membership, others? Where are the expiry dates kept today?
8. How far ahead do you start a renewal? Has anything expired without anyone noticing?
9. Do you track expiries for each client employee, or only for the company?

## Headcount and bands

10. How do you know each client's current headcount? How often does it change, and who tells you?
11. Who checks that the band on the invoice matches the real headcount?

## Business Setup, Maintenance and GM Representative

12. Business Setup runs through milestones (35/35/15/15). Who decides that a milestone is reached, and who tells Finance to invoice it?
13. What does Company Maintenance include month to month?
14. For GM Representative: what does the representative do, and what do you track?

## Handovers

15. With Recruitment and Employer of Record hires: at what point does a new hire's visa or mobilization come to you, and with what information?
16. With Payroll: which changes do you share (new joiners, leavers, iqama details)?

## Employer of Record (shared with Payroll)

17. For Employer of Record employees, where does your part end and Payroll's begin? Who keeps the employee's details up to date?

## Language

18. Would anyone on your team work better with the app in Arabic? Which screens matter most: the request queue, the client page, the documents?

## The Director's view

19. On a Monday morning, what do you want to see at a glance? What would you hand to one of your team as their list for the day?

**Likely first piece to build** (to confirm): the client document expiry calendar plus a request queue per client.
