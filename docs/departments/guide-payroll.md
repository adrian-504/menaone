# Interview guide: Payroll

For the conversation with the Director of Payroll. Ask the shared questions in [README.md](README.md) as well.

**What we think the department does** (to be corrected in the conversation): runs each client's monthly payroll. That covers the employee list, changes, GOSI, the salary-transfer file, payslips and end-of-service. It also pays the employees MENA employs under Employer of Record. Clients pay a monthly retainer banded by headcount, using the cumulative band rule the owner confirmed on 14 September 2026.

## The monthly run

1. Walk us through one client's run, from the first day of the month to salaries paid. What happens on which date?
2. Where is each run prepared: which payroll program, Excel, or GOSI and the bank directly?
3. How many runs are there each month, and who on the team does which?
4. What do you need from each client every month (joiners, leavers, overtime, deductions), and by when? How late does it usually arrive?
5. What happens when something arrives after the cut-off?

## Employees

6. What do you hold for each employee, and where? Which of it is sensitive (salary, ID, bank details)?
7. Employer of Record employees: how is their run different, and who approves it? Admin & PRO handles their visas and iqamas. Where does your part begin, and what do you need from them?

## Headcount and bands

8. Your run gives the real headcount for each client every month. Who uses that number today, and how does it reach Finance for the invoice?
9. Do the headcount you pay and the headcount you invoice ever differ? Why?

## Handovers

10. With Admin & PRO: which employee changes do you exchange (iqamas, new joiners)?
11. With Finance: what do you send each month, and in what form?

## The Director's view

12. At a glance, what do you want to see: this month's runs by client, which clients are waiting on inputs, what's late?

**Likely first piece to build** (to confirm): the monthly run calendar (status by client) and headcount recorded each month. Payroll itself stays in the payroll program.
