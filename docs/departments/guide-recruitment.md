# Interview guide: Recruitment

For the conversation with the Director of Recruitment. Ask the shared questions in [README.md](README.md) as well.

**What we think the department does** (to be corrected in the conversation): fills client vacancies. Recruitment Advisory is paid as a percentage of the annual package, usually about 10% and varying by client and staff type. Dedicated Recruiter is a monthly retainer. Hires can go to the client's payroll or through Employer of Record. The 90-day replacement guarantee applies.

## Job orders

1. How does a vacancy arrive, and what do you need from the client before you start: job description, salary range, nationality, location?
2. How many vacancies are open right now? How many do you usually handle in a month?
3. Who on the team takes which vacancies?

## Candidates

4. Where do candidates come from: LinkedIn, job boards, your own database, referrals? Which recruitment software or spreadsheet holds them?
5. Which stages does a candidate go through, from first contact to their first day? What are the stages called in your team?
6. What do you send the client, and in what format? How does the client give feedback?
7. How long does a typical vacancy take to fill? Where does it stall?
8. Do you keep candidates you didn't place for future vacancies? For how long, and with their consent?

## Placement and fee

9. When is a placement final: offer accepted, first day, or end of probation?
10. How is the fee worked out, and who tells Finance to invoice it?
11. The 90-day replacement: how often does it happen, and how do you track the 90 days?

## Handovers

12. With Employer of Record hires: what do you pass to Admin & PRO (visa, mobilization) and to Payroll, and when?
13. What comes from Sales when a client signs, and what's missing from it?

## The Director's view

14. At a glance, what do you want to see: open vacancies, candidates at each stage, anything stalled, placements this month?

**Likely first piece to build** (to confirm): each client's job orders with the stage each candidate is at. Its shape is close to the opportunities board.
