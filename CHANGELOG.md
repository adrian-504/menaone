# MENA One — changes

One entry per install, newest first: the version, the day it was installed, and in two lines what changed.
Settings → Data shows the last five. `scripts/release-check.mjs` refuses a build whose version has no entry here.

## 1.50 — 2026-09-30
Your data is safer: each day's backup is also copied to OneDrive (last 14 kept), and the database is checked every time the app opens.
Settings → Data shows the version, backups and check. On a sent proposal, "Client asked for changes…" sits in Follow-up; a revision's button reads Mark revision sent.

## 1.49 — 2026-09-29
Company page as a dossier: details, people and what to remember on the left; where we stand, what's next and recent on the right.
Meetings are grouped by day with the next one opened up; the meeting page shows the client beside your notes.

## 1.48 — 2026-09-29
Follow-ups in one click (email, call, WhatsApp, met, or the client's reply); template emails from the company page's Email button.
Notes no longer flicker when you flip between them, and a Clients list gathers every note about a client.

## 1.47 — 2026-09-29
"Client asked for changes" turns a sent proposal into a revision of the same proposal, and follow-up counts from the latest send.

## 1.46 — 2026-09-29
Meeting notes are findable: search shows where a word matched, meeting lists show a line of the notes, and Notes has "From meetings".

## 1.45 — 2026-09-28
Accountancy is two all-inclusive packages, Startup and Active.

## 1.44 — 2026-09-28
Undo a proposal review recorded by mistake; a deck you made yourself in the client folder links to its proposal.

## 1.43 — 2026-09-28
The Business Setup and Maintenance package is a proposal template with two options.
